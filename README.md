# Sales-Performance-Analysis-KPI-Dashboard-Excel-365

## Objective
Cleaned raw data and built an interactive pivot table and pivot chart dashboard to track sales KPIs.

## Dataset
provided by IIM skills as part of the data analytics master course. Raw dataset not uploaded due to course terms.

## Tool Used 
Excel 365 (pivot tables, pivot charts)

## key Insights
1. **West is the top region**, contributing about 30% of total sales (~249M),
   while *Northeast is the weakest* at about 17% (~138M).
2. **Wholesale drives about 54% of total sales** (~445M), while Export contributes
   only about 15%, showing heavy dependence on a single channel.
3. **Monthly sales stayed fairly stable through the year**, with a low in February,
   a peak around August to October, and a slight decline in November and December.
4. **Top 10 products have very close sales (2.48M to 2.72M)**, with Product 180 leading.
   Revenue is not dependent on any single product.
